📧 Phishing Awareness

Phishing is a trick used to steal personal information through fake emails, messages, or websites.

  • Check the sender's email address.
  • Be careful with urgent messages.
  • Verify links before opening them.
  • Never share passwords or OTPs.

🔐 How to Spot Phishing

  • Look for spelling mistakes.
  • Be careful with unexpected prizes.
  • Verify suspicious links.
  • Do not download unknown attachments.

🔑 Password Safety

  • Use strong, unique passwords.
  • Never share your password.
  • Enable two-factor authentication.
  • Do not reuse the same password everywhere.

⚠️ Warning Signs to Remember

  • Unexpected links asking for passwords or OTPs.
  • Misspelled or unusual domain names.
  • Links containing misleading symbols or addresses.
  • HTTPS alone does not prove a website is trustworthy.

🧰 Cyber Safety Toolkit

Simple habits to protect yourself online.

🔐 OTP & Two-Factor Authentication

  • Never share your OTP with anyone.
  • Never approve an unexpected login request.
  • Enable two-factor authentication on important accounts.
  • Use an authenticator app or passkey when supported.

📶 Public Wi-Fi Safety

  • Confirm the correct Wi-Fi network with the venue.
  • Avoid sensitive transactions on untrusted networks.
  • Use HTTPS websites and keep your device updated.
  • Turn off automatic connection to unknown networks.
